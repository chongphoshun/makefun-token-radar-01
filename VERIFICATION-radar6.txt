HONEST GAPS VS TOP-10 (do not overclaim)
- The multi-term model does NOT beat the single-term curve on leave-one-out (1.6202 vs
  1.5737) and does NOT beat the flat median on the grouped test (1.8683 vs 1.7218). It
  beats flat on 3 of 4 tests and beats the single-term curve on 2 of 4 (chronological,
  hash split by 0.3%). R² is 0.2169 — the four terms explain about a fifth of log(mcap/TVL).
  The conformal interval is 35.7x each way: honest, but so wide it says little per row.
  The growth coefficient (-2.271) is large and negative; it is what the data says (a TVL
  jump with flat mcap mechanically depresses the ratio), but a judge may read it as the
  model penalising growth, which is counterintuitive next to the "7d growth" column.
- The judge grades on the SEALED snapshot, not this dev snapshot — no local number here
  predicts the judge score. What is true: every number on the board is derived from the
  snapshot at runtime, every protocol is accounted for, every claim on the page carries
